# Third-party materials

The MIT license in `LICENSE` applies to the original source code in this
archive. It does not relicense model checkpoints, benchmark evaluators, or
benchmark prompt text created by third parties.

The files under `prompts/` are immutable snapshots of the public GenEval,
DPG-Bench, and GenEval2 generation prompts used in the paper. They are
included solely to identify the evaluated inputs and preserve ordering. Their
original upstream terms and citations continue to apply. The archive includes
no model weights, evaluator checkpoints, generated images, generated answers,
or per-prompt scores.
